# Andy's Visible Villager Trades 0.2.0

- Shows Minecraft's generated future villager tiers without unlocking or altering trades.
- Adds the included empty-hand crouch-interact Villager Details panel.
- Shows read-only carried inventory and live entity status.
- Records bounded, qualified interaction, damage, nearby death, curing-start, and transformation observations after installation.
- Adds operator settings through `AdminTradeControll` and cheats-free BDS console commands.
- Ships as one `.mcaddon` with reciprocal linked Behavior and Resource Packs.
- Includes achievement-friendly declarations, Vibrant Visuals support, and revised Andy-and-villager artwork.
